# Laser Fish

Laser Fish is  a small Scratch game made in less than 24 hours for [SEAJAM](https://itch.io/jam/seajam)

[Github](https://violet.github.io/Laser-Fish/) [Itch.io](https://violet78910.itch.io/laser-fish) [Scratch](https://turbowarp.org/598608328?fps=60)

Orginal Scratch Code has been complied to HTML/CSS/JS

## How to Play
- You can move up or down with arrow keys/ W & S
- You can shoot lasers out of your eyes by clicking the space key but you need to wait 5 seconds in between shots
- You gain points by shooting the garbage
- You lose by getting hit by a piece of garbage
